# Getting Started

## Placement

Place the **Bulk Cell Marker** anywhere on your ME network. It requires a channel and draws
2 ae/t while idle. Right-click the block to open its GUI.

The marker discovers cells through the network, so the block itself has no inventory of its own:
mount your [MEGA Cells](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/mega-cells) Bulk Cells in
drives, ME chests, or cell docks as usual, and they will show up in the marker automatically.

## The GUI

The GUI has its own panel in the AE2 style. Each of the 63 slots in the main grid represents one mounted
bulk cell on the current page, in a fixed order. Use the arrow buttons in the left toolbar to
page through when there are more than 63 cells. See [Marking Cells](marking-cells.md) for details.
