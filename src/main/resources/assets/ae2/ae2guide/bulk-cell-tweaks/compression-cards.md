# Compression Cards

Compression cards change how a bulk cell handles compressed variants of its stored item. The
marker installs and removes them without opening the cell dock.

## Installing

Hold a **compression card** on your cursor and **right-click** the slot of the cell you want to
install it into. One card is taken from the stack; the rest stays on your cursor.

## Removing

**Right-click with an empty hand** on a cell's slot to take its compression card back. The card
appears on your cursor.

Installing or removing a card re-mounts the cell, so the storage reconfiguration takes effect
immediately.
